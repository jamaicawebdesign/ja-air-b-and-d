Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026. This is a plain-language summary; please have it reviewed by a legal professional before relying on it.

What we collect

Your name, email, business details and the property, reservation and guest records you enter. We also keep your chosen plan and payment status. We do not store bank card numbers.

How we use it

To run your account, show your dashboard, send messages you configure, verify payments and provide support.

Who can see it

Only you can see your records; this is enforced in our database. We use Supabase to host data and Vercel to host the website. We do not sell your data.

Guest data

If you enter guest details, you are responsible for using them lawfully. Booking requests made on your direct website are delivered to you.

Your rights

You may export your data from Settings, correct it, or ask us to delete your account. Under the Jamaica Data Protection Act you may ask what we hold about you.
