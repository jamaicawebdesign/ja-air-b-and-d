Help centre

Quick answers to common questions.

Getting started

How do I begin? Create an account, add your properties with rates, then connect your channels from the Channels page.

How do I get verified? After signing up, pay for your plan by bank transfer using the details on the thank-you page, then send proof of payment on WhatsApp. We verify within one business day.

Calendars & channels

How does sync work? Paste each platform's iCal link into Havenstay, and paste your Havenstay export link back into the platform. Platforms refresh every few hours, so sync is not instant. Havenstay flags any overlap it finds.

Direct booking website

How do I publish it? Open the Website page, choose a web address and brand name, mark properties as listed and switch the site on. Guests send booking requests, and you confirm them.

Do guests pay online? Not yet. You confirm the request and arrange payment directly.

Plans & billing

How do plans work? Plans are based on how many properties you manage (Starter 2, Host 6, Pro 15, Manager 40). See pricing. You can see your plan under Settings.

How do I renew? Pay by bank transfer and send proof on WhatsApp.

Account & data

Can I export my data? Yes, from Settings choose Download my data. You can also change your password and email there.
