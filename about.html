About Havenstay

Built in Jamaica, for Jamaican hosts.

Havenstay helps landlords, villa hosts and property managers run their rentals from one dashboard. We bring your Airbnb, Vrbo and Booking.com calendars, guest messages, cleaning tasks, owner reports and a direct booking website into one place, so you avoid double bookings and keep more of every booking.

Why we built it

Most rental software is priced and designed for large international managers. Local hosts told us they wanted something simple, affordable and friendly to how business is done here, including paying by bank transfer and talking to a real person on WhatsApp.

What we believe

Simple tools you can learn in an afternoon. Fair pricing with no commission on your bookings. Private data that only you can see.
