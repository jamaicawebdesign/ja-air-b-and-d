Questions

Good to know

How do calendars sync with Airbnb, Vrbo and Booking.com?

Using each platform's iCal calendar link. You paste theirs into Havenstay, and paste your Havenstay link back into theirs. Platforms refresh these every few hours, so it isn't instant, and Havenstay flags any overlap it sees.

Can I manage properties for other owners?

Yes. Assign an owner and a management fee to each property, log expenses, and generate a printable monthly statement showing revenue, fees and net payout.

Do my guests pay online?

Not yet. Guests send a booking request on your website and you confirm it and arrange payment with them directly.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your data is stored in Supabase with row-level security, so each account can only ever read its own records.

What does it cost?

Plans start at US$12 a month for 1–2 properties. Create your account, then pay by bank transfer to get verified.
